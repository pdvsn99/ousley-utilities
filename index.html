What We Do

Specialist services for every stage of your connection

From first application to energisation, we handle liaison with your Distribution Network Operator, paperwork, and coordination — taking the headache off your hands entirely.

DNO Application & Feasibility

We manage the full connection application from first contact with the Distribution Network Operator through to confirmed design and accepted offer. We handle the paperwork, chase progress, and keep you informed throughout.

Temporary Builder's Supplies

Need power on site before the permanent connection is in? We arrange temporary supplies from application through to disconnection once the main supply is established — coordinated alongside your build programme.

Site Readiness Reviews

Before anything gets booked, we review photos, meter positions, clearances, backboards, and isolator space. Catching issues at this stage saves abortive visits, delays, and costs further down the line.

Through to Energisation

We stay with the job to the end — coordinating meter installation, supplier registration, and final energisation so your property is live and ready on the day it needs to be.

What's Involved

A meter move requires sign-off from both your supplier and the network operator. We manage both sides — raising the job, confirming technical requirements, and arranging the works — so nothing falls through the gap between the two.

Minimising Disruption

We schedule meter moves to align with your build or renovation programme, coordinating disconnection and reconnection windows to keep downtime as short as possible and the project on track.

Upgrades

Modern homes increasingly demand more from the grid — EV chargers, heat pumps, underfloor heating, and high-load appliances all add up. We manage the upgrade process end-to-end, ensuring your supply matches what the property actually needs.

Downgrades

Oversized supply capacity carries ongoing standing charges. Where a property's demand is lower than its current supply rating, we can manage a downgrade to bring costs into line — handled cleanly with the network.

After Energisation

The job doesn't always end when the supply goes live. Billing queries, supplier registration issues, and account discrepancies are common — and we're on hand to resolve them so you don't have to navigate supplier systems yourself.

A Retained Point of Contact

For developers and landlords managing multiple properties, we act as a single point of contact for all account-related queries across a portfolio — consistent, informed, and already familiar with the history of each connection.

When Things Have Gone Wrong

Connection processes break down in predictable ways — rejected applications, miscommunication between supplier and network, stalled jobs with no clear owner. We come in, establish what's happened, and take it from there.

Escalation & Resolution

We know how DNOs, suppliers, and metering agents operate, and where accountability sits in each process. That knowledge means we can escalate effectively and get stalled jobs moving again — without starting from scratch.

How It Works

Five stages, one point of contact

Connection projects go wrong in the handovers — between supplier, network operator and metering agent. We hold all of them, so there's never a gap for your project to fall into.

Stage 01

Initial Conversation

Tell us about the property and what you need it to do. No jargon, no obligation — just a clear view of what's involved and whether we're the right fit.

Stage 02

Feasibility & Site Review

We establish what the network can offer, what capacity the property actually needs, and whether the site is ready — before anything gets committed or booked.

Stage 03

Application & Offer

We raise the application with the DNO, manage the paperwork, and review the quotation with you — including what's contestable and what isn't.

Stage 04

Coordination & Works

Scheduling around your build programme, chasing progress, and keeping supplier, network and metering agent aligned so dates hold.

Stage 05

Energisation & Aftercare

Supply goes live, registration is confirmed, and we stay available afterwards for account queries and anything that surfaces later.

Already mid-project? You don't need to start at stage one. We regularly pick up connections that have stalled part-way through — a rejected application, a job with no clear owner, or works booked against the wrong site information. Tell us where things stand and we'll take it from there.

A Distribution Network Operator owns and maintains the cables, substations and infrastructure that carry electricity to properties in a given region. They are not your supplier. Any new connection, meter move or change in supply capacity has to be approved and delivered by the DNO for your area — which is why so much of a connection project comes down to dealing with them effectively.

It depends on what the network has to do. A straightforward connection where there's existing capacity in the street moves considerably faster than one requiring a new substation, a road crossing, or reinforcement of the local network. Wayleaves, permissions from third-party landowners and local authority road permits are the usual causes of delay. We give you a realistic view at feasibility stage rather than an optimistic one — and we tell you early if something looks likely to slip.

The DNO sets the connection charge, and it varies enormously depending on distance from the existing network, the capacity required, ground conditions, and whether any reinforcement is needed. Some of that cost may be contestable — meaning parts of the work can be competitively tendered rather than carried out by the DNO itself. We help you understand the quotation you've been given, what is and isn't contestable, and whether the figure looks right.
